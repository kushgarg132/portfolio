# Surface: portfolio home (app/page.tsx)

Mode: Experience (portfolio), recruiter skim first. Single scrolling page.
Audience/job: recruiters judge fit in <1 min, then download resume or contact; engineers dig into projects/source.
Proof: real stats ($600M XPAY savings, 7+ SWIFT message types, 3 live systems), 4 projects with live/source links, 3 achievements. No fabricated claims.
Constraints: keep profile photo in hero, keep Formspree contact form. Light/dark toggle dropped (one committed theme). Avoid: flashy/slow motion, generic dev template, too artsy to skim, heavy on mobile. Motion is live in-page (no video).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is printed like a security document: a payments engineer's portfolio issued as a banknote/bond. Refuses the dark-navy-plus-accent dev template with a split hero and card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Tinted note-stock ground (not cream), deep intaglio ink, guilloche rosettes and borders in two rainbow-printed tints, microprint rules, serial numbers in one vermilion ink reserved for the figure under pressure, a windowed metallic security thread. Didone engraved legend, extended grotesque caps, wide mono serials.

STORY: In one viewport the visitor reads who (Kush Garg), what (Backend + AI systems engineer, StoneX payments), proof (denominations with their source lines), and acts (resume / contact on the serial row). Below: statement, ledger of experience, issued series of projects, instruments, honours, remittance-slip contact form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One note face filling the viewport inside a guilloche frame. Left third: engraved line portrait in a guilloche oval. Right: KUSH GARG at legend scale, role line, three denominations each with its source line. Bottom band: red serial number plus Download resume (primary) and Contact, socials. Microprint runs the border.

FORM: Security-printed note (grounded list position 4 of 7; seed a8303c1f). Signature motion: guilloche rosettes draw themselves once on load; security thread fills with scroll as progress; sections print in on one axis, in sequence. Raises: Jacquard (every headline figure traces to its source line), Versailles (single axis, sequenced reveals), Futurist book (single accent ink for the figure under pressure), Algorave (links show their destination before commit).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
